Start your music journey!

Despite wide availability and easy access to the internet, making music still seems like an overly intimidating hobby to get started with. That is the issue this website will try to help you with! My goal here is to make producing music feel natural, and to emphasise your enjoyment over anything!

On this website, you’ll find basic starting information on how to set up a music program and how to start making your first song! In just a few minutes you’ll be ready and set up. We’ll also help you through a basic demo of a first song so you get an idea of what to expect and can get familiar with the program. It’s likely much simpler than you think it is!

Take a look through the navigation, or click on the pages from here! If you’re new to the website, start at Intro. If you’re coming back, welcome back™ — jump to where you left off. You can also leave a message through the contact form.
